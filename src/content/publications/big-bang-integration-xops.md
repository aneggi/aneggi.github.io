Authors: Alessandro Aneggi, Xiaozhou Li, Andrea Rosani, Andrea Janes, Oswald Lanz.

Analyzes a multi-partner project's integration failure, including delayed integration, limited observability, and differences between environments. Describes Integractor, an actor-centric early-integration approach evaluated through scenario-based architectural analysis and a simulated integration environment.
