Authors: A. Aneggi, V. Stoico, A. Janes.

Analyzes controlled experiments on ten antipatterns, showing that performance degradation does not always imply higher instantaneous power draw, helping separate latency problems from true energy inefficiencies.

Related preprint: [Performance Antipatterns: Angel or Devil for Power Consumption?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12079).
